Every project starts somewhere. Sometimes it begins with a carefully drawn plan. More often, it starts with a question that refuses to leave you alone: what if we tried this?

Cerulean begins with that kind of question. We’re making a space to explore ideas, build things, and share what we learn in the process. This notebook is the first small step.

## Room for curiosity

It’s easy to rush from an idea to an answer. We want to leave a little more room in between: room to look closely, to test an assumption, and to notice the unexpected detail that changes how you see the problem.

That doesn’t mean staying in the abstract. The most interesting questions tend to become clearer when you make something. A small prototype can reveal more than a long discussion. An experiment that doesn’t work can still point toward a better question.

> Start with a question. Make something small. Pay attention to what it teaches you.

## A notebook, in the open

We’ll use this space to share ideas in progress, notes from experiments, and reflections on the things we build. Some posts will be practical. Others will be an invitation to think about something with us.

We want the writing to be useful even when the work is unfinished. That means explaining what we tried, what happened, and what we’re still unsure about. A clear account of a small discovery is worth sharing.

## What comes next

For now, we’re starting simply: a place to gather our thoughts and a way to meet people who are curious about similar things. The shape of what follows will come from the work itself, and from the conversations around it.

If something here sparks an idea, or you’re working on a question you’d like to share, write to us at [hi \[at\] cerulean.works](mailto:hi@cerulean.works).

Thanks for being here at the beginning.

— Cerulean
